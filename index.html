About

What I do

I'm an IT and support professional, and what I lead with is people. The technical range is real — I support and secure systems, and I build the tools that make them run better — but the part that's hardest to teach is the part I'm best at: reading people, staying composed under pressure, and making technical things make sense to anyone across the table.

Today I'm an Administrative Assistant in the Commissioner's Office at NYC HRA/DSS, the first point of contact for visitors and senior leadership, staying alert to social engineering attempts to protect the floor's safety and confidentiality. Before that I spent two years supporting Hunter College's Department of Public Safety, operating enterprise access-control systems for 200+ people a day and building the department's first digital training platform.

I'm looking to join a team in an IT, systems, or analyst role — at a law firm, a financial institution, or a tech company — where supporting people well and building the tools that make the work easier both matter.

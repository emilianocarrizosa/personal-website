About

Ambitious professional, emerging Security Engineer.

Technical expertise means very little if it cannot serve the people relying on it. As a B.A. in Computer Science graduate and CompTIA A+ certified IT professional, I pair a strong technical foundation in software development, infrastructure support, and systems security with years of front-line customer service experience.

By integrating human psychology into my technical workflow, I bring a unique advantage to security and support teams, whether that means identifying subtle social engineering patterns or translating complex technical roadblocks for non-technical stakeholders.

Where I am now

Today I’m an Administrative Assistant in the Commissioner’s Office at NYC HRA/DSS, gatekeeper and first line of defense for the Commissioner’s Office and the executive and senior staff floor: the first point of contact for visitors and senior leadership, delivering white-glove service while thinking like a security engineer: anticipating risks before they become incidents and protecting the floor’s safety and confidentiality. Before that I spent two years supporting Hunter College’s Department of Public Safety, operating enterprise access-control systems for 200+ people a day and building the department’s first digital training platform.

I’m looking to join a team in an IT, systems, or analyst role, at a law firm, a financial institution, or a tech company, where supporting people well and building the tools that make the work easier both matter.

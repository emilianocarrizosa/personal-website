Overview

Hunter College’s Visitors Center needed a way to train and certify its staff. Public Safety commissioned me to build it, and I built it on my own: the department’s first digital training and certification platform, a secure desktop application that runs offline on the Visitors Center’s Windows 10 Enterprise workstations.

What it does

Training and certification

Staff work through training modules, are tested on a bank of 39 questions tagged by category (including accessibility and compliance topics), and receive a formal certificate on completion.

Administration

Administrators can edit the text of the notices and documents staff see, review how balanced the question bank is across categories, and grant a retake for a single module without resetting everything else.

Guided tours

A reusable tour component walks new staff through the application, and a separate tour does the same for administrators.

How I built it

The interface is React and the application logic is Rust, packaged as a desktop app with Tauri. Records live in a local SQLCipher database, so the data is encrypted on disk and the application works without a network connection.

It deploys to the department’s Dell Latitude workstations running Windows 10 Enterprise, and I resolved the hardware-specific issues that came up along the way.

Rollout

I presented the project to senior leadership, including the CIO, and secured approval. I then deployed it, trained the staff who would use it, and gave the college’s IT team a handoff document.
